# FirstStep — Proposed Feature Requirements

**Status:** Independent concept; not validated or approved by Charles Schwab.

## Product objective
Explore whether optional, contextual investing education improves understanding and confidence for first-time adult investors without encouraging unnecessary trading.

## MVP feature candidates
| ID | Feature | User need | Example acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| US-01 | Contextual glossary | Understand unfamiliar investing terms | A user can open a plain-language explanation of ETF, stock, diversification, and potential loss without leaving the current learning step. |
| US-02 | Guided first-investment education | Understand choices and risks | A user can voluntarily open a short lesson and return to the prior screen; no securities are recommended. |
| US-03 | Learning preferences | Choose relevant topics | A user can select topics, skip selection, and revise preferences later. |
| US-04 | Optional knowledge checks | Check comprehension | A user can answer or skip non-punitive questions and see explanatory feedback. |

## Requirements and safeguards
- Entire experience is optional, accessible, and skippable.
- Content is plain-language and includes balanced explanations of risk and potential loss.
- No individualized investment advice, personalized securities recommendations, trade execution, guaranteed-return claims, or pressure to invest.
- Privacy, security, legal, compliance, content accuracy, and accessibility reviews are prerequisites to release.

## Out of scope
Personalized portfolio recommendations; suitability assessments; automated trade execution; gamified incentives for placing trades.

## Dependencies and open questions
- Which educational features are already available in the current Schwab experience?
- Where would contextual education be genuinely additive rather than duplicative?
- Which audiences would opt in, and which would prefer to skip?
- What compliance and disclosure requirements apply to the proposed content?
